Multi-Temporal SAR-Based Crop-Type Mapping

Sehore District, Madhya Pradesh | M.Sc. Geoinformatics Assignment

Project Overview & Methodology

This web application presents a multi-temporal crop classification workflow developed for the Sehore District spanning the Kharif and Rabi agricultural seasons (June 2025 – April 2026). Using Sentinel-1 Ground Range Detected (GRD) synthetic aperture radar (SAR) data in descending orbit (VV and VH polarizations), time-series backscatter trajectories were captured across 12-day composite intervals.

A total of 82 predictor bands—including gap-filled temporal windows, polarization differences, and seasonal statistics (mean and standard deviation)—were extracted using field reference polygons buffered to align with the 10-meter pixel grid. A supervised Random Forest classifier (100 trees) was trained to distinguish between Wheat and Soybean phenological cycles.
